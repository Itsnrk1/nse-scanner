STRATEGY (3-minute candles)
Read on the previous day, the latest completed session. Candles are labelled by start time: the last 3-minute candle is 15:27 and the second last is 15:24. A stock matches when the 15:24 and 15:27 candles have the same trend and the 15:24 volume is greater than the 15:27 volume.
Trend = candle colour (close vs open); a flat candle has no trend. Direction trades with the common trend: up is LONG, down is SHORT (with the default rule).
Entry: the 09:15 open on entry day. Exit: 15:27.

Data notes: Yahoo has no 3-minute feed, so 1-minute candles (about 7 days of history) are downloaded and combined into 3-minute candles on a 09:15 grid: open = first, high = max, low = min, close = last, volume = sum of the 1-minute candles in the bucket. A candle with no trades is missing from Yahoo's data and can only block a match. INCOMPLETE means no completed session (or neither of the two candles) came back. STALE means the previous day differs from most other symbols.
