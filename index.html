STRATEGY (1-minute and 3-minute candles)
Read on the previous day, the latest completed session. Candles are labelled by start time: the last 1-minute candle is 15:29 (second last 15:28); the last 3-minute candle is 15:27 (second last 15:24). A stock matches only when ALL three hold:
1) 1m: the 15:28 and 15:29 candles have the same trend, and the 15:28 volume is greater than the 15:29 volume.
2) 3m: the 15:24 and 15:27 candles have the same trend, and the 15:24 volume is greater than the 15:27 volume.
3) The 1m 15:28 candle has the same trend as the 3m 15:24 candle.
Trend = candle colour (close vs open); a flat candle has no trend. Direction trades with the common trend: up is LONG, down is SHORT (with the default rule).
Entry: the 09:15 open on entry day. Exit: 15:27.

Data notes: 1-minute candles come straight from Yahoo's 1-minute feed (about 7 days of history). 3-minute candles are built from them on a 09:15 grid: open = first, high = max, low = min, close = last, volume = sum of the 1-minute candles in the bucket. A candle with no trades is missing from Yahoo's data and can only block a match. INCOMPLETE means no completed session (or none of the four candles) came back. STALE means the previous day differs from most other symbols.
