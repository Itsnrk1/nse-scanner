STRATEGY (15-minute and 5-minute candles)
Read on the previous day, the latest completed session. Candles are labelled by start time: the last 15-minute candle is 15:15 (second last 15:00); the last 5-minute candle is 15:25 (second last 15:20). A stock matches only when ALL THREE hold:
1) 15m: the 15:00 and 15:15 candles are in opposite trends (one Up, one Down).
2) 5m: the 15:20 and 15:25 candles are in opposite trends.
3) The 15m 15:00 candle and the 5m 15:20 candle have the same trend.
Trend = candle colour (close vs open); a flat candle has no trend, so it is never opposite or the same. Direction follows the final candles (15m 15:15 / 5m 15:25): Up is LONG, Down is SHORT.
Entry: the 09:15 open on entry day. Exit: 15:27.

Data notes: Candles come straight from Yahoo's 15-minute and 5-minute feeds. A candle with no trades is missing from Yahoo's data and can only block a match. INCOMPLETE means no completed session (or none of the four candles) came back. STALE means the previous day differs from most other symbols.
