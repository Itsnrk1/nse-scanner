STRATEGY
Everything is read on the previous day, the latest completed session.
1. 5-minute candles: the last two candles (15:20 and 15:25) trend in different directions, and the 15:20 candle's volume is greater than the 15:25 candle's.
2. 15-minute candles: the last second candle (15:00) trends the same way as the 5-minute 15:20 candle.
Direction follows the 15:20 trend: up is LONG, down is SHORT.
Entry: the 09:15 open on entry day. Exit: 15:27.

Data notes: The 5-minute and 15-minute candles are each fetched from Yahoo's own feed for that timeframe; nothing is calculated from another timeframe. A candle with no trades is missing from Yahoo's data and has no trend, so it can never satisfy a trend condition, which means missing data can only block a match, never create one. INCOMPLETE means no completed session came back. STALE means the previous day differs from most other symbols.
